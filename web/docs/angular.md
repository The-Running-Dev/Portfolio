# Angular Projects

A collection of Angular-based applications and demos:

- **BarStrad**: Angular based menu application, with theming, language support, orders, and Discord notifications and a separate menu Discord bot. [GitHub](https://github.com/The-Running-Dev/BarStrad)
- **Manual Tests**: Line of business app for recording manually tested equipment. [GitHub](https://github.com/The-Running-Dev/Contec-ManualTests)
- **NetAssure Client**: Network hardware management, Dockerized, Salesforce integration. [GitHub](https://github.com/The-Running-Dev/Demo-NetAssure/tree/master/web-client/src)
- **PatientViewer.NG**: Simple Angular app to display a list of patients. [GitHub](https://github.com/The-Running-Dev/Demo-PatientVIewer.NG)
- **Repairs**: Business app for recording repairs to equipment. [GitHub](https://github.com/The-Running-Dev/Contec-Repairs)
- **Rock Paper Scissors**: Angular implementation of the classic game, with a twist. [GitHub](https://github.com/The-Running-Dev/Demo-RockPaperScissors)
