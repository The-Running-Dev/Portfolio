## 🟡 Intermediate


### 7. [NestJS Fundamentals Course (Free)](https://docs.nestjs.com/first-steps)

- Official tutorial series using TypeScript, REST, and CRUD. Goes into architecture, middleware, pipes, filters, interceptors, etc.

### 8. [NestJS Zero to Hero (Udemy, by Ariel Weinberger)](https://www.udemy.com/course/nestjs-zero-to-hero/)

- Deep dive into advanced topics like GraphQL, microservices, testing, REST, Auth (JWT), MongoDB, TypeORM, deployment. ~15 hours of content.

### 9. [NestJS: The Complete Developer’s Guide (Udemy, by Stephen Grider)](https://www.udemy.com/course/nestjs-the-complete-developers-guide/)

- Covers Auth, PostgreSQL, testing, deployment. Great for practical, project-based learning.

### 10. [“Mastering NestJS” Blog Series by Wanago](https://wanago.io/tag/nestjs/)

- Explains concepts like custom decorators, advanced validation, database integration, and authentication step-by-step.

### 11. [LogRocket: Getting Started with NestJS](https://blog.logrocket.com/getting-started-with-nestjs/)

- Beginner-friendly blog post to get started quickly.

### 12. [Better Programming (Medium): Real-world NestJS Projects](https://medium.com/better-programming)

- Search for "NestJS" for real-world project writeups and tips.

### 5. [NestJS Zero to Hero on Udemy (by Ariel Weinberger)](https://www.udemy.com/course/nestjs-zero-to-hero/)
